# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, available in full at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

The short version, which is not a substitute for reading it: everyone
participating here — in issues, in pull requests, in review — is expected to
behave decently. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not tolerated,
and neither is the style of technical argument that is really about the person
making it.

Disagreement is fine and expected; this repository is full of decisions with
reasons attached precisely so they can be argued with. Argue with the reason.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers, through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/thienanblog/geda-transfer/security/advisories/new)
if there is no other channel available to you. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this document in good
faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's guidelines: a private warning
first for a first lapse, up to a permanent ban for sustained or severe
behaviour.
